i'm sick of social media. i uninstalled it from my phone in... march? early march. it took some adjusting, it felt like i was missing out on people's lives, but i always reminded myself how little i actually get out of it. it's just people sending me reels, people sharing their activism, and most often, people sharing their mundane moments. i had to take a step back and say to myself "why the hell do i care about any of this?".

I sunk so many hours into scrolling until my eyes got sore. I was reaching for my phone like it was a cigarette.. i still reach for it but now that there's no dopamine-portal it's a lot easier to put it back down. Another thing I noticed was that the lack of dopamine response from my phone lead to me leaving my phone in other rooms, since there's no more reason to keep it on me all the time.

Counter-intuitively, something that helps is how shit the web experiences of these platforms are. I think it actually helped break the addiction.

I still have my little treat: linkedin. But it's way less addictive than instagram or tiktok because of how fucking boring it is. Usually i'm on there to see if anyone has a new job, or launched their startup, or published a blog post, but that's rare so I'm not spending a lot of time. Oh and checking if recruiters are trying to reach me.

once you step away you realise it all SUCKS. there's NOTHING there other than drivel to keep you entertained just long enough to view the next ad...
