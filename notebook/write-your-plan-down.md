Often I want to achieve things that take multiple weeks or even months of work to achieve.

For example, losing weight, getting my 5K time down to 25min, or improving my cycling until I can keep up with the intermediate group at 30km/h avg.

My brain feels like a leaky bucket, if I try to keep everything in my head then i'll lose sight of the goal.

You need a plan written down for when your brain or your willpower fails. It's a lot easier to refer to a plan than it is to memorise and recall everything.
