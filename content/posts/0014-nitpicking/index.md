A nitpick is a mirage. It's pointing out a "problem", but upon closer inspection, the requested change delivers no meaningful improvement to the work.

Nitpicking is hostile. It's treating someone as if they can't be trusted.

It's possible to nitpick a project to death. It's sabotage.

At the very least, nitpicks should be non-blocking. In our software world that seems to be the case already.
